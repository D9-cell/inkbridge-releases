See it in action

Three ways to use your phone with your computer. Switch between them any time from the top of the phone screen.

Draw or sign on the phone; the stroke appears on your computer, with pressure from a stylus.
Write a word, pause, and it is typed into whatever is open on your computer. Other readings are one tap away.
Your computer's screen, live on the phone: tap to click, hold to right-click, swipe to scroll, and type with either keyboard.

Everything it does

In plain words. Most of it works from the web page too; the few things that need the Android app say so.

CreateDraw and write

Pen tabletUse the phone like a drawing tablet for any app on your computer. Great for signing documents and sketching.
Handwriting into textWrite with your finger; InkBridge reads it and types the words into the app that's open, like a keyboard.
Undo and alternativesTyped the wrong word? Undo it, or tap one of the other readings it offers.

ControlUse your computer from the sofa

Screen on your phoneSee your computer's screen live and use it: tap to click, hold to right-click, drag, and swipe to scroll.
Keyboard and shortcutsA full computer keyboard on the phone, your phone's own keyboard, and one-tap buttons for copy, paste, undo and switching windows.
Volume and windowsChange the volume, mute the microphone, and jump straight to any open window from a list.

Look after itYour laptop, from your pocket

Lock, sleep, restart, shut downOne tap from the phone menu, wherever you are in the room.
Unlock with your fingerprintUnlock the computer by confirming with your phone's fingerprint or face.Linux & Windows
Turns on and off by itselfSet when the laptop wakes up in the morning and shuts down at night, and change it from the phone.
Alerts on your phoneCharger unplugged, battery low, lid opened, going to sleep: the phone tells you, even with the page closed.
Distance modeLeaving the laptop for a bit? If someone touches its keyboard, touchpad or lid, your phone knows right away.
Travel modeLaptop in your bag on your phone's hotspot: it saves battery, stays cool and keeps working with the lid closed.
Locks when you walk awayWalk off with your phone and the laptop locks itself after a few seconds.Android app

ShareBetween phone and computer

Files both waysSend a photo or document to the computer, or pick any file on the computer and get it on your phone.
Shared clipboardCopy on one, paste on the other. Text you copy moves across in a tap.
Phone as headsetHear your computer's calls and sound on the phone and talk through it.Android app

ConnectAnywhere, privately

Cable, Wi-Fi or away from homeA USB cable, your home Wi-Fi, or Tailscale when you're out: it picks the best way by itself.
Web page, no app neededOn iPhone, or any phone, just scan the code: your computer serves a page that works like an app.
Updates by itselfNew versions download and install on their own, only the one for your system, checked before installing.

How it works ↓

How to start

About two minutes, once.

Install on your computerPick your system above and open the download.
Choose PairInkBridge shows a QR code and a 6-digit code.
Scan it with your phoneThe Android app, or simply the web page it opens: nothing to install on iPhone.
Private by design

No account and no cloud. Your phone talks straight to your computer over a USB cable, your Wi-Fi or Tailscale, encrypted end to end.
