How it works ↓

What it does

One app on your computer, your phone in your hand.

Pen tabletDraw and sign on your computer with your phone, with stylus pressure.
Handwriting → textWrite on the phone; the words are typed into whatever app is open.
Screen remoteSee your computer live on the phone; tap, scroll, drag and type.
Laptop menuLock, sleep, shut down, set it to turn on and off, and unlock with your fingerprint.
Files & clipboardSend files and copied text both ways in a tap.
AlertsBattery, charger, lid and "someone touched it" alerts, even with the page closed.

How to start

About two minutes, once.

Install on your computerPick your system above and open the download.
Choose PairInkBridge shows a QR code and a 6-digit code.
Scan it with your phoneThe Android app, or simply the web page it opens: nothing to install on iPhone.
Private by design

No account and no cloud. Your phone talks straight to your computer over a USB cable, your Wi-Fi or Tailscale, encrypted end to end.
